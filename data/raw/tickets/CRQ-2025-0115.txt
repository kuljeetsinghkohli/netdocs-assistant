TICKET_ID: CRQ-2025-0115
TITLE: vSmart and vBond Software Upgrade to 20.12.1
STATUS: Planned
PRIORITY: High
RISK_LEVEL: High
AFFECTED_DEVICE: vSmart-01 (10.200.0.10), vSmart-02 (10.200.0.11), vBond-01 (10.200.0.30), vBond-02 (10.200.0.31)
AFFECTED_SITE: LON-DC01 (management plane)
CHANGE_DATE: 2025-01-15
CHANGE_WINDOW: 2025-01-15 00:00 UTC – 2025-01-15 04:00 UTC
REQUESTED_BY: Alex Mercer
APPROVED_BY: Sandra Lin
IMPLEMENTED_BY: Alex Mercer (planned)
TICKET_TYPE: Major Change

DESCRIPTION:
vManage was upgraded to 20.12.1 in CRQ-2024-1101. This change upgrades vSmart and vBond to the same version (20.12.1) to restore component version parity. Mixed-version operation (vManage 20.12.1, vSmart/vBond 20.9.3) is supported per Cisco compatibility matrix but is not the target state.

During vSmart upgrade: OMP sessions will be briefly disrupted per controller (30–60 seconds). vEdge devices retain cached OMP policy and continue forwarding during this window. BFD-based failover and data plane remain unaffected.

During vBond upgrade: New ZTP registrations will be unavailable. Existing devices are not affected.

IMPLEMENTATION PLAN:
1. Pre-change: Take VMware snapshots of all 4 controller VMs.
2. Upgrade vBond-02 (secondary): Administration > Software Upgrade.
3. Verify vBond-02 on 20.12.1; vBond-01 still on 20.9.3 — ZTP still functional.
4. Upgrade vBond-01.
5. Upgrade vSmart-02 (secondary):
   - Monitor OMP session reconvergence after upgrade restart (~60 s).
   - Confirm all vEdges reconnect to vSmart-01.
6. Upgrade vSmart-01.
7. Verify both vSmarts on 20.12.1 and all vEdge OMP sessions reconnected.
8. Delete snapshots after 48-hour stabilisation.

ROLLBACK PLAN:
1. Revert VM snapshot for any failed controller.
2. Confirm OMP sessions re-establish with previous version controller.
3. Open TAC case if upgrade repeatedly fails.

TEST PLAN:
- All 4 controllers on 20.12.1.
- All vEdge devices have OMP sessions to both vSmarts (Up).
- vManage shows version parity across all controllers.
- ZTP test: stage a new test device through vBond to confirm ZTP still works.

REFERENCES: CRQ-2024-1101, DD-001, Cisco vSmart Upgrade Guide
