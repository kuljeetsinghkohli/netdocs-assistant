TICKET_TYPE: Major Change

DESCRIPTION:
OT/IoT VPN (VPN 3, 192.168.100.0/22) is currently only present at LON-DC01 and EMEA branches. The Contoso OT team is deploying new IoT sensors at NYC and SGP manufacturing facilities, requiring VPN 3 to be extended to NYC-DC01 and SGP-DC01 sites.

Changes required:
1. Create VPN 3 routing instance on NYC-DC01-RTR01 and SGP-DC01-RTR01.
2. Update vManage device templates to add VPN 3 interface on APAC/Americas vEdges.
3. Update centralised policy to permit OT/IoT traffic within VPN 3 (no internet, no cross-VPN).
4. Add OT subnet ranges to IPAM: NYC OT = 192.168.101.0/24, SGP OT = 192.168.102.0/24.

Risk assessment: High — modifying VPN segmentation on hub routers. Error could cause cross-VPN leakage (OT to Corporate). All changes must be validated in test environment first.

IMPLEMENTATION PLAN:
[In progress — see sub-task tracking in ITSM]
1. Test in lab environment (lab vEdge at LON-DC01 lab rack) — COMPLETE.
2. Apply to NYC-DC01-RTR01 in change window.
3. Apply to SGP-DC01-RTR01.
4. Push updated vManage policy.
5. Validate OT connectivity (IoT sensor ping test from OT VLAN).
6. Validate no cross-VPN leakage (ping Corporate VPN1 from OT VPN3 — must fail).

ROLLBACK PLAN:
1. Remove VPN 3 interface from NYC and SGP DC routers.
2. Revert vManage templates to previous version.
3. Remove IPAM entries for NYC/SGP OT subnets.
NOTE: Rollback will leave IoT devices offline — coordinate with OT team before execution.

TEST PLAN:
- OT subnet 192.168.101.0/24 reachable from NYC OT sensors.
- Ping from 192.168.101.x to 10.10.0.1 (Corporate) FAILS.
- Ping from 192.168.101.x to 192.168.100.1 (LON OT hub) SUCCEEDS.
- Zabbix monitoring: new OT hosts added and showing green.
