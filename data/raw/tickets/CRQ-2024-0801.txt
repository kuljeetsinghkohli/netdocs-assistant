AFFECTED_SITE: LON-DC01

TICKET_TYPE: Standard Change

DESCRIPTION:
BGP keepalive and hold-timer values on LON-DC01-RTR01 are currently set to 60/180 seconds (keepalive/hold). During a recent BGP flap incident (INC-2024-0721), the hold timer duration of 180 seconds meant that a brief transport outage caused an extended BGP session disruption (3 minutes). The investigation identified that aligning timers to the Contoso standard (30/90) and enabling BFD fallover would improve failover time from ~3 minutes to sub-second.

This change aligns BGP timers for the BT MPLS peer (172.16.0.1) and Lumen backup peer (172.16.1.1) with the Contoso standard. BFD fallover is also enabled.

IMPLEMENTATION PLAN:
1. SSH to LON-DC01-RTR01 (10.200.1.1 via jump host 10.200.5.1).
2. Enter BGP configuration:
   router bgp 65001
    neighbor 172.16.0.1 timers 30 90
    neighbor 172.16.0.1 fall-over bfd
    neighbor 172.16.1.1 timers 30 90
    neighbor 172.16.1.1 fall-over bfd
3. Verify BGP sessions re-establish (soft reset not required for timer change — takes effect at next negotiation or session reset).
4. Confirm BFD sessions are established: show bfd neighbors.
5. Monitor BGP summary for 15 minutes.

ROLLBACK PLAN:
Revert BGP timers to previous values (60/180) and remove fall-over bfd:
   router bgp 65001
    neighbor 172.16.0.1 timers 60 180
    no neighbor 172.16.0.1 fall-over bfd
    neighbor 172.16.1.1 timers 60 180
    no neighbor 172.16.1.1 fall-over bfd

TEST PLAN:
Post-change validation:
- show ip bgp neighbors 172.16.0.1 | include Hold time|Keepalive
- Confirm: Hold time = 90, Keepalive = 30
- show bfd neighbors
- Confirm BFD sessions in Up state for both peers

OUTCOME: Successful. BGP sessions re-established with correct timers within 2 minutes. BFD sessions established. No traffic impact observed.
